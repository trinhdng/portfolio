Altano Bank: an 8-page Power BI report for three audiences

After cleaning the data, the bank's spend was only about 43% of its $10M monthly target, and 13.7% of cards had an undisclosed brand, which made market-share figures unreliable.

Problem

What Victorian Airbnb guests praise and complain about

Yarra Ranges guests were the most satisfied (mean sentiment 0.86) and Monash the least (0.69); amenities were the most common complaint, and value complaints used the strongest language.

Problem

Hosts and platform managers had no clear view of where satisfaction was lowest, what drives complaints, and how guest priorities differ by room type.

What I did

  • Merged review and listing data, then scored sentiment with VADER across the 10 most-reviewed neighbourhoods.
  • Defined dissatisfaction with a justified threshold and measured aspects (cleanliness, location, amenities, value) using keyword analysis and correlation.
  • Used LDA topic modelling (5 topics) to compare Entire home and Private room guests.

Recommendations

  • Cleanliness and communication audits for lower-scoring areas such as Monash.
  • Targeted 10-15% coupons for underperforming listings.
  • Room-type coaching: location guidance for Entire home hosts, communication training for Private room hosts.

FreshBake Bakery: simulating end-of-day discounts to cut waste

A 40% discount cut waste from 23.7 to 8.9 loaves a day and reduced the average loss, but at 150 loaves neither discount made a profit, so production and pricing also need review.

Problem

How should the bakery set its end-of-day discount when demand is uncertain, balancing profit, waste and sustainability (UN SDG 12)?

What I did

  • Built an Excel decision model with four random inputs: Normal demand, discount-dependent Normal demand, Uniform ingredient cost and Binomial defects.
  • Ran 1,000-trial Monte Carlo simulations for 20% and 40% discounts, with convergence checks.
  • Compared profit distributions, loss probabilities (80.2% vs 85.7%) and waste risk.

Recommendation

Adopt the 40% discount, then test production quantity and cost structure.

48 months of returns for 9 ASX stocks

Portfolio optimisation with Solver: three models, one recommendation

The unconstrained model put 100% in two stocks. A six-stock integer model gave up only 0.18 points of monthly return versus the best linear model while staying diversified.

Problem

Recommend an investment portfolio for a client across Health Care, Financials and Information Technology, using risk and sector rules.

What I did

  • Calculated returns and risk, then grouped assets into three risk bands using natural gaps in the data.
  • Built LP, ILP and NLP (Sharpe ratio) models in Excel Solver and read the LP sensitivity report.
  • Compared all three and explained the trade-off between return and diversification.

Recommendation

The ILP portfolio: six funded assets, 5-20% each, an expected 1.46% monthly return.
